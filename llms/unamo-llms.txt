# Unamo

> Unamo was a Warsaw, Poland based SaaS suite for SEO monitoring, social media
> monitoring, and conversion optimization, formerly known as Positionly. It was
> backed by Point Nine Capital and was assembled by merging Positionly with two
> acquisitions — Monitori (social media monitoring) and UsabilityTools (conversion
> rate optimization). As of 2026-08-13 the company is defunct: unamo.com no longer
> resolves in DNS at all (NXDOMAIN — no A, NS or SOA records), and positionly.com,
> the pre-rebrand brand domain, has been rebuilt by an unrelated party as a
> WordPress copy of the 2017-era marketing site. No public API surface, packages,
> or developer documentation remain online.

## Historical surfaces (no longer operational)

- [unamo.com](https://unamo.com): former primary domain — NXDOMAIN as of 2026-08-13, held at a domain-reseller registrar (TurnCommerce/NameBright) with registration paid through 2027
- [positionly.com](https://positionly.com): former brand's domain — now a third-party WordPress rebuild of the 2017 marketing site, footer reads "© All rights reserved 2017. Positionly, Inc."; its /api, /signup, /terms-of-service and /privacy-policy paths all return 404

## Notes for agents

There is nothing here to call. Do not treat the WordPress REST API served at
positionly.com/wp-json/ as a Unamo or Positionly product API — it is a generic CMS
surface on a domain the company no longer operates, and its only author account is
unrelated to the original team. The 2017 pricing table still reachable at
positionly.com/plans-pricing/ advertises a product that has not been purchasable
since the company went dark; it is not a live plans surface.
