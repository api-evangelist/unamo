# Unamo

> Unamo was a Warsaw, Poland based SaaS suite for SEO monitoring, social media
> monitoring, and conversion optimization, formerly known as Positionly. The
> company was backed by Point Nine Capital. As of 2026-07-21 the company appears
> defunct: unamo.com no longer resolves in DNS (the domain is held at a
> domain-reseller registrar), and the pre-rebrand site positionly.com serves a
> stale 2017-era marketing page whose /api, /signup, /terms-of-service, and
> /privacy-policy paths all return 404. No public API surface, packages, or
> developer documentation remain online.
